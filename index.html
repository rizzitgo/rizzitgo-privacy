Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the RizzitGO Discord Bot ("the bot", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, how long it is kept, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Data We Collect

The bot collects only the minimum data needed to operate its features:

  • Discord user ID — a numeric identifier used to track lottery entries, enforce entry limits, and deliver messages such as welcome messages and prize notifications.
  • Parcel / order numbers — submitted voluntarily by users when entering the Milestone Lottery, used to verify eligibility against our own platform.
  • Lottery activity data — associated with an entry, including a timestamp, the milestone number, the parcel's status, and, for winners, the draw date.

We do not collect or store message content, presence/status information, email addresses, or Discord profile data.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the Milestone Lottery: verify parcels, track and count entries, enforce per-user entry limits, prevent duplicate submissions, run draws, and notify winners.
  • To send automated welcome messages to new members.
  • To distribute and restore coupons. Coupon and coupon-restore requests are processed in real time through the RizzitGO API and are not stored by the bot.
  • To process the Spin feature. Spin requests, including requests involving valid shipped parcels and extra spins, are processed in real time through the RizzitGO API. Information required to process the Spin request is used only to verify the request and process the corresponding result or coupon.
  • Spin results and related redemption information are delivered to the user through Discord Direct Messages (DMs). The information exchanged for this purpose is not stored by the bot after the request has been processed.

3. Data Sharing

We only communicate with the RizzitGO website API (our own platform) to verify parcels and process coupons and Spin requests. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any unrelated third-party companies for commercial purposes.

4. Data Retention

  • All stored data is automatically deleted within 30 days. An automated daily process permanently removes any lottery entry or winner record older than 30 days.
  • Pending lottery entries are also cleared after each milestone draw is completed.
  • Real-time coupon, coupon-restore, Spin, and welcome-message features do not retain data in the bot after the request has been processed.

5. Data Security

Data is stored securely on our own infrastructure and is protected using encryption at rest. Access is limited to authorized operators of the bot.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time. Because all data is automatically deleted within 30 days, most data is removed without any action on your part, but you can request immediate deletion by contacting us.

7. Contact

  • Email: support@rizzitgo.com
  • Discord: through our official RizzitGO Discord server support channels

By using the bot, you consent to this Privacy Policy. We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
