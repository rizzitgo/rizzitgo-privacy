Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the RizzitGO Discord Bot ("the bot", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, how long it is kept, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Data We Collect

The bot collects only the minimum data needed to operate its features:

  • Discord user ID — a numeric identifier used to track lottery entries, enforce entry limits, and deliver messages such as welcome messages and prize notifications.
  • Parcel / order numbers — submitted voluntarily by users when entering the Milestone Lottery, used to verify eligibility against our own platform.
  • Lottery activity data — associated with an entry, including a timestamp, the milestone number, the parcel's status, and, for winners, the draw date.
  • Email address — collected when using the Spin feature through a private Discord Direct Message (DM). The email is used to identify and verify the user's RizzitGO account and to process Spin requests.

We do not collect or store message content, presence/status information, or Discord profile information such as usernames, avatars, bios, or other profile details beyond the Discord user ID required to operate the bot's lottery features.

Email-related information is handled through private Discord Direct Messages (DMs) and is not publicly displayed in Discord channels.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the Milestone Lottery: verify parcels, track and count entries, enforce per-user entry limits, prevent duplicate submissions, run draws, and notify winners.
  • To send automated welcome messages to new members.
  • To distribute and restore coupons. Coupon and coupon-restore requests are processed in real time through the RizzitGO API and are not stored by the bot.
  • To process the Spin feature. Spin requests, including requests involving valid shipped parcels and extra spins, require an email address to identify and verify the corresponding RizzitGO account and to process the requested Spin.
  • Email addresses collected for the Spin feature are stored securely in our database so that the bot can identify the user's RizzitGO account and process future Spin requests without requiring the user to provide the information each time. The email address is used only for this purpose and is not sold or shared with unrelated third parties.
  • Spin results and related redemption information are delivered to the user through Discord Direct Messages (DMs).

3. Data Sharing

We only communicate with the RizzitGO website API (our own platform) to verify parcels and process coupons and Spin requests. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any unrelated third-party companies for commercial purposes.

4. Data Retention

  • Active lottery entries are cleared after each milestone draw is completed.
  • Lottery winner records, including the winner's Discord user ID, winning parcel number, milestone number, draw date, and total number of entries, are retained for 12 months after the relevant draw for historical and reference purposes and may be viewed through the bot's winners history. After this period, these records are permanently deleted.
  • Email addresses collected for the Spin feature are retained in our secure database for as long as they are required to support the Spin feature.
  • The bot's !unlink function can be used through a private Discord Direct Message (DM) to remove the stored email address from the database. Users may also contact RizzitGO support to request deletion of their stored email address.
  • Real-time coupon, coupon-restore, and welcome-message features do not retain data in the bot after the request has been processed. Spin requests may retain the user's email address as described above.

5. Data Security

Data is stored securely on our own infrastructure and is protected using encryption at rest. Access is limited to authorized operators of the bot.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us through our official support channels.

For email addresses stored for the Spin feature, users can use the !unlink command through a private Discord Direct Message (DM) to remove the stored email address directly from the bot's database. Users may also contact RizzitGO support if they need assistance with deleting their stored email address or other personal data associated with the bot.

When a deletion request is received, we will review the request and take appropriate steps to remove the requested data from our active systems, subject to any information that may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, legal, or historical purposes as described in this Privacy Policy.

Our official Discord support channels are the fastest way to contact us, and we aim to respond as quickly as possible. Requests sent by email to support@rizzitgo.com may take up to 48 hours to receive a response.

7. Contact & Response Times

  • Discord: through our official RizzitGO Discord server support channels.
  • Email: support@rizzitgo.com
